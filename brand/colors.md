# Ringside color tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B0B0F` | Wordmark, text, dark panels |
| Paper | `#F4F5F8` | Light page background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and dark-surface wordmark |
| Periwinkle | `#8B9BFF` | One small accent in the mark and controls |
| Muted | `#646A78` | Supporting text |

Use the light logo on white or Paper. Use the dark logo on Ink. Keep clear space of at least half the mark width around the lockup. Do not rotate, gradient-fill, or animate the mark like a spinner.

Typography: Geist Sans Regular 400 and Bold 700. Font files and license are in `fonts/`.
